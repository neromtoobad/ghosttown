## The idea
Ghost Town is a **town you walk around**. Every building is a feature, and every feature teaches one Zcash idea by making you play it. The Mafia table is the heart of the town. Glasstown is just a district across the river: a short side trip, never the front door.

## New features
1. **Three Lanterns:** the Watcher hides a coin under one of 3 lanterns and shows a SHA-256 commitment first. They shuffle, you pick, he reveals the salt, and your browser re-hashes it. Streak counter. Once per game the Watcher tries to cheat by claiming another lantern, and the commitment check catches him live.
2. **Auditor's Desk:** 5 suspects, a ledger of 8 shielded payments (all noise), and 3 viewing keys found in the night. Each key opens only its own payments. Find the payment from the Watcher (memo "for services rendered") and accuse its recipient. Three accusations allowed. The puzzle is new each time (seeded).
3. **Arcade:** Dark Pool full-screen, with a local best and a "beat the drones" streak.
4. **Ghost Radio:** a small player for the song on the map. Off by default; one tap plays it.
